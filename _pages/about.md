Welcome to my homepage!

I am a PhD candidate in the [Department of Statistics](https://stat.uchicago.edu) at the [University of Chicago](https://www.uchicago.edu), advised by Professor Wei Biao Wu. I also received my master's degree from the same department. Before coming to Chicago, I earned my bachelor's degree from the [School of Mathematical Sciences](https://math.fudan.edu.cn) at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn), where I was advised by Professor Shuqin Zhang. In the summer of 2019, I was a research intern at [Harvard University](https://www.harvard.edu), working with Professor Junwei Lu.


My research focuses on stochastic optimization, quantile regression, and modern machine learning. I am particularly interested in the theoretical foundations and methodology of uncertainty quantification for online learning and optimization algorithms. I also collaborate on problems in causal inference and single-cell clustering.
