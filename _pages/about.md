Welcome to my homepage!

I am a PhD candidate in the [Department of Statistics](https://stat.uchicago.edu) at the [University of Chicago](https://www.uchicago.edu), advised by Professor Wei Biao Wu, and I also received my master's degree here. Before coming to Chicago, I earned my bachelor's degree from the [School of Mathematical Sciences](https://math.fudan.edu.cn) at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn), where I was advised by Professor Shuqin Zhang. In the summer of 2019, I was a student research intern at [Harvard University](https://www.harvard.edu), advised by Professor Junwei Lu.

My research focuses on stochastic optimization, quantile regression, and modern machine learning, particularly the theoretical foundations and methodology of their uncertainty quantification. I also collaborate on causal inference and single-cell clustering modelling.
